## Built

### Klini

2026 · In production · https://klini.app/

Patients book, move and cancel their own appointments by texting the clinic's WhatsApp in plain Spanish. No menus, no phone tree.

Live at Centro Médico CEPEM in Buenos Aires and in clinics across seven countries. It never diagnoses or recommends medication. Writing to a real clinic's calendar is the part that took a summer.

A sample exchange, in Spanish, as shown on the site:

> Patient: Hola, ¿cuándo tengo el turno de la vacuna?
> Klini: El jueves 21 a las 10:30, en vacunatorio.
> Patient: ¿Lo puedo pasar a la tarde?
> Klini: Listo, lo moví al jueves 21 a las 16:00. Te confirmo por acá.
> Status: Turno reprogramado · jue 21 · 16:00

### SellWant

2026 · Live · https://sellwant.com

A marketplace with two kinds of listing: what people are selling, and what people are trying to buy. A want and a sale meet in the middle and lock in.

Running on the Austin ticket scene first: ACL weekends, formals, Halloween. Preset categories and structured browsing instead of a feed you have to scroll.

### PayPal

2026 · Internship

Rebuilt the front end of PayPal's instant commerce links, the buttons a merchant uses to sell something from a photo, a price and a link.

Owned it from low-level design through a staged production ramp. Those buttons move more than a million dollars a month.

### wa-slack

2026 · In production

A WhatsApp inbox that lives inside Slack. A customer message arrives as a thread in a channel the support team already has open, and the reply goes straight back out to WhatsApp.

One thread per ticket, one channel per company, and a dashboard for whoever is working the queue. Built at Texarg, carrying real conversations.

### Texarg

2022 · Running · https://texarg.com

Custom automation for local businesses: appointment reminders, lead routing, document processing, billing. I map where a business is losing hours, then build the thing that stops it. Not templates.
